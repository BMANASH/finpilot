# FINPILOT

A dynamic, personalized financial planning engine that generates tailored cash-flow waterfalls, multi-tier risk capacity profiling, and goal-based asset allocation.

---

## Overview

Most traditional financial planning tools rely on rigid, one-size-fits-all heuristics (such as a generic 50/30/20 rule or age-based asset splits). FINPILOT replaces static formulas with an adaptive, state-driven financial hierarchy that prioritizes safety, liquidity, and goal funding before long-term capital allocation.

The platform dynamically calculates:
- **Cash-Flow Allocation:** How monthly surplus should be prioritized across essential expenses, risk protection, emergency reserves, debt management, and investing.
- **Investment Allocation:** How surplus capital should be deployed across asset classes based on time horizon, risk capacity, and liquidity demands.

---

## Core Financial Framework

FINPILOT processes user financial profiles through a sequential financial priority hierarchy:

1. **Cash Flow & Stability:** Evaluates baseline income stability, essential living expenses, and net surplus capacity.
2. **Financial Protection:** Assesses health coverage, personal accident protection, and dependency-driven term life insurance requirements.
3. **Liquidity & Emergency Reserve:** Calculates custom emergency fund targets based on monthly liabilities, job stability, and family dependencies.
4. **Debt Optimization:** Categorizes liabilities by cost (high, medium, low) to optimize debt repayment schedules versus investment returns.
5. **Goal-Based Planning:** Evaluates goals across short-term (0–3 years), medium-term (3–7 years), and long-term (7+ years) horizons, resolving timing and funding conflicts.
6. **Retirement Planning:** Computes inflation-adjusted corpus requirements and projected withdrawal sustainability.
7. **Asset Allocation:** Builds diversified portfolio strategies based on objective risk capacity rather than subjective risk tolerance alone.

---

## Architecture Principles

- **Deterministic Mathematics:** All Time Value of Money (TVM), amortization schedules, and portfolio metrics are computed using deterministic mathematical engines.
- **Explainable Analytics:** Every allocation, recommendation, and score transparently displays the underlying formulas, inputs, and assumptions.
- **Scenario Planning:** Supports sensitivity testing across base, conservative, and growth market scenarios.
